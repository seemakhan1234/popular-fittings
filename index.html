Carbon Steel Blind Flange vs. Plate Flanges & Industrial Flange Types

Selecting the right carbon steel flange depends on operating pressure, temperature, fluid medium, and maintenance access. As dedicated plate flange manufacturer and suppliers of carbon steel blind flange, we help buyers match each flange profile to the exact job requirement.

Carbon Steel Blind Flange (IS 2062 / A105)

Solid discs without a center bore used to seal pipe ends, valves, and pressure vessel nozzles. Carbon steel blind flange make inspection, line isolation, and future system expansion straightforward while handling high bolt loads.

Plate Flange (IS 2062 / A105)

As experienced plate flange manufacturers, we cut and machine flat-faced and raised-face plate flanges from certified steel plates. They offer dependable strength and weldability for water lines, HVAC, ducting, and general engineering.

Weldneck & Slip-On Flange (A105/LF2)

Weldneck carbon steel flange feature a tapered hub that reduces stress concentration in high-pressure and high-temperature lines, while slip-on flanges simplify alignment in moderate-duty piping systems.
