Carbon Steel Blind Flange

Our heavy-duty carbon steel blind flange are solid forged or plate-machined discs engineered to seal pipe ends, valve outlets, and pressure vessel nozzles. Built for high-pressure containment and simple line isolation during maintenance or hydro-testing, every carbon steel blind flange meets strict dimensional tolerances.

Standards: ANSI B16.5 / ASME Grades: ASTM A105 / IS 2062 Class: 150 to 1500 Facing: RF / FF / RTJ

IS 2062 & Carbon Steel Plate Flange

As established plate flange manufacturer in Mumbai and Thane, we produce precision CNC-drilled MS and carbon steel plate flange for water treatment plants, HVAC lines, fire-fighting networks, and structural piping. Available in standard IS 6392, BS 4504, and custom client drawings.

Carbon Steel Weldneck Flange

Designed with a long tapered hub that transfers stress smoothly to the pipe wall, our carbon steel weldneck flange prevent warping and joint fatigue under extreme temperature fluctuations and high-pressure service in oil, gas, and chemical plants.

Standards: ANSI B16.5 / B16.47 Grades: ASTM A105 / A350 LF2 Schedules: SCH 10 to SCH XXS

Carbon Steel Slip-On Flange

Our carbon steel slip-on flange slide directly over the pipe end for fast alignment and double-fillet welding. They provide an economical, leak-resistant connection for low-to-medium pressure industrial lines and utility headers.

Standards: ANSI B16.5 / DIN / IS Grades: ASTM A105 / Mild Steel Class: 150 / 300 / 600
