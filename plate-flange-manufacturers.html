Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a Plate Flange and a Slip-On Flange?

A plate flange is cut from flat steel plate without a raised hub, making it economical and ideal for low-to-medium pressure water, air, and structural lines. A forged slip-on flange includes a hub collar for added reinforcement in higher-pressure piping.
