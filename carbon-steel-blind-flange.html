Precision-machined carbon steel blind flanges built to ANSI B16.5, ASME, ASTM A105, and IS 2062 specifications for high-pressure pipeline isolation, hydro-testing, and pressure vessel closures across India.

Heavy-Duty Carbon Steel Blind Flanges (ASTM A105 / IS 2062)

Popular Fittings is a leading carbon steel flange and carbon steel blind flange manufacturer in Mumbai and Thane. Unlike standard bore flanges, a blind flange is a solid disc engineered without a center hole to seal the ends of piping systems, valves, and vessel nozzles.

Because blind flanges absorb substantial static pressure and bolt stresses—especially in larger diameters—our carbon steel blind flanges are manufactured from certified forged ASTM A105 billets and high-tensile IS 2062 steel plates with accurate serrated facing finishes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Carbon Steel Blind Flange used for?

It is used to close off the end of a pipeline, valve, or vessel opening permanently or temporarily, making future maintenance, cleaning, or line extension simple without cutting pipework.
